# AI tells — the subtract-only list

Condensed from the `humanizer` skill (a verbatim port of Wikipedia's *Signs of AI
Writing*, WikiProject AI Cleanup — the one source the whole field cites). Load the
full `humanizer` skill when you want every before/after example.

## ⚠️ Read this first — take the subtraction, leave the addition

`humanizer` has two halves:
1. **Remove AI patterns** (the list below) — ✅ **use all of it** here.
2. **Add "personality and soul"** — first-person "I", casual words ("stuff",
   "things"), jokes, deliberate messiness, "I genuinely don't know how to feel
   about this" — ❌ **do NOT use it here.**

Ken's docs are formal peer-register framing (see `gold-standard.md`). Tina and
Foundry contain **none** of that essay-blog voice. If you add it, you break the
register you're trying to hit. **Your job is only to remove the tells — the
"human voice" you're aiming at is the gold standard, not a personal blog.**

## The removals (apply all)

### Vocabulary — cut on sight
delve · tapestry · testament · underscore · pivotal · landscape (abstract) ·
showcase · seamless · robust · vibrant · crucial · foster · intricate/intricacies ·
realm · navigate (figurative) · ever-evolving · additionally · align with ·
enhance · garner · interplay · key (adjective filler) · valuable

### Significance inflation
"marks a pivotal moment", "reflects a broader shift", "stands as a testament to",
"setting the stage for". → State plainly what happened.

### Promotional / advertisement tone
boasts a · nestled · in the heart of · groundbreaking · breathtaking · must-have ·
rich (figurative) · profound. → Neutral, factual.

### Superficial -ing tails
"…, highlighting the platform's flexibility", "…, underscoring its importance",
"…, ensuring scalability", "…, reflecting the team's focus". → Delete; they add
fake depth. If the clause matters, make it a real sentence.

### Copula avoidance
"serves as / stands as / represents a / functions as" where **is/are** is truer.
Foundry deliberately writes "The agent state **is** the continuity layer". Use is.

### Negative parallelism & tailing negation
"It's not just X, it's Y" · "It's not merely a … , it's a …" · trailing "…, no
guessing" / "…, no wasted motion". → Write the positive claim as a real clause.

### Rule of three
Forcing ideas into triads to sound comprehensive ("innovation, inspiration, and
insight"). A true pair or a true four is fine — cut the *manufactured* third.
(Note: a real, load-bearing triad like "harness, model, and agent state store" is
legitimate — the test is whether the third item is real or padding.)

### Elegant variation (synonym cycling)
"the protagonist … the main character … the central figure … the hero". → Pick one
noun and repeat it.

### False ranges
"from the Big Bang to the cosmic web, from birth to death of stars" where the
endpoints aren't a real scale. → List the actual items.

### Filler & hedging
"in order to" → "to" · "due to the fact that" → "because" · "at this point in
time" → "now" · "has the ability to" → "can" · "it is important to note that" →
(cut). Kill stacked hedges: "could potentially possibly" → "may".

### Persuasive-authority tropes
"The real question is", "at its core", "in reality", "what really matters",
"fundamentally", "the heart of the matter". → Usually just restates an ordinary
point with ceremony. Cut the ceremony.

### Signposting / announcements
"Let's dive in", "let's explore", "let's break this down", "here's what you need
to know", "without further ado". → Just make the point.

### Vague attributions / weasel words
"Industry reports", "Experts argue", "Observers have noted", "some critics" with
nobody named. → Name the source or cut the claim.

### Formatting tells
- **Em-dash overuse** — most rewrite cleanly as commas, periods, or parentheses.
- **Mechanical boldface** — bolding phrases for emphasis in every bullet.
- **Inline-header vertical lists** — "**Performance:** we made it faster" bullets.
- **Title Case In Headings** → sentence case.
- **Emojis** in headings/bullets → remove.
- **Curly quotes** → straight quotes.
- **Fragmented headers** — a heading followed by a one-line restatement of the
  heading before real content. Cut the warm-up line.

### Chatbot artifacts (when text was pasted from a chat)
"Great question!", "Certainly!", "You're absolutely right!", "I hope this helps",
"Let me know if…", "as of my last training update". → Delete entirely.

## After the removals

Re-read one paragraph against a `gold-standard.md` excerpt. If it now reads
*plainer* than the anchor (you stripped too hard and left novice-flat prose),
restore precision with concrete verbs and payoffs from the register table — **not**
with essay-blog personality. The target is always the peer register.
